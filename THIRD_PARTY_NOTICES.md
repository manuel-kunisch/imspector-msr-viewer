# Third-party notices

## 3Dscript

The 3D view of the MSR Viewer (`msr_volume.py`, `msr_volume_view.py` and `msr_volume_panel.py`) is a Python
port of the interactive volume renderer of the Fiji plugin **3Dscript** by Benjamin Schmid,
<https://github.com/bene51/3Dscript>, adapted and without the animation language. Portions of it (rendering
algorithms, transfer functions, opacity correction, shader structure and mouse interaction) are derived from
3Dscript and are used under the license below.

If you use the 3D view in your work, please also cite 3Dscript:

> Schmid B, Tripal P, Fraaß T, Kersten C, Ruder B, Grüneboom A, Huisken J, Palmisano R. 3Dscript: animating
> 3D/4D microscopy data using a natural-language-based syntax. *Nat Methods* 16, 278–280 (2019).
> <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0359-1>
